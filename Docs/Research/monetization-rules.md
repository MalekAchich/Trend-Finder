# Monetization rules (for the future Socials page)

**Researched:** 2026-10-07. Platforms change these often: re-check each one before relying on it, and show the "checked on" date in the app.

**Why this matters for us:** our videos are an AI character recreating trends with Kling. Every platform now pays only for **original** content and requires **labels on realistic AI**. Re-uploads, templated mass production and unlabelled AI people are what gets demonetized or suppressed.

## At a glance

| Platform | Program | Main thresholds | Where it's available |
|---|---|---|---|
| YouTube (Shorts) | YouTube Partner Program | 1,000 subscribers + 10M valid Shorts views in 90 days (or 4,000 watch hours in 12 months). Basic tier: 3M Shorts views in 90 days or 3,000 hours. | Long country list. **Tunisia is included.** |
| TikTok | Creator Rewards Program | 18+, 10,000 followers, 100,000 views in the last 30 days, Personal account; only videos **over 1 minute** earn | Few countries (US, UK, DE, FR, JP, KR, BR, plus some recent additions). **Not Tunisia.** |
| Instagram | Gifts, Subscriptions, Live badges, invite-only Reels bonuses | Professional account, 18+; Gifts around 500 followers, Subscriptions and badges around 10,000 | Full set in the US, UK, CA, AU and the EU. Limited elsewhere: check the account's Professional dashboard. |
| X | Original Content Rewards (replaced Creator Revenue Sharing on 2026-09-08) | X Premium, 500 verified followers, 500,000 verified Home-timeline impressions in 90 days, a review of 10 original posts | Payouts went through Stripe (country-limited). Check whether that's still the route. |

## YouTube
- **Partner Program:** 1,000 subscribers plus either 10M valid public Shorts views in the last 90 days or 4,000 public watch hours in 12 months. The lower "expanded" tier (fan funding, no ad share) needs 500 subscribers plus 3M Shorts views in 90 days or 3,000 hours. Since January 2026 the calculation also weights engagement, not only raw views.
- **Inauthentic content (monetization policy, clarified July 2026):** generic, repetitive or template-based content can't earn. That includes mass-produced AI videos, "off-putting" content, and AI personas discussing sensitive topics such as health or finance. Enforcement is three strikes: a warning, a 90-day suspension, then removal from the Partner Program.
- **What stays monetizable:** AI used as a tool, with real human creative input and disclosure. The line is "replacement, not assistance".
- **Labels:** realistic synthetic people, voices or scenes must be disclosed with the "altered or synthetic content" setting. Since May 2026 YouTube also auto-labels detected photorealistic AI. On Shorts the label is an overlay on the video. A label alone doesn't reduce reach or earnings; hiding AI repeatedly can cost the Partner Program.
- **For us:** each recreation needs a visible creative twist (character, setting, script), and the "altered or synthetic" box must always be ticked.

## TikTok
- **Creator Rewards:** 18+, 10,000 followers, 100,000 video views in the last 30 days, a Personal (not Business) account in good standing, and residence in an eligible country with a local payout method. Only original videos **longer than 60 seconds** earn. Duets, stitches, reposts and lip-syncs to copyrighted music don't qualify.
- **AI content:** realistic AI people, voices or scenes must carry TikTok's AI-generated label (TikTok also auto-labels detected AI). AI content can earn when it is original, transformative and labelled. Deepfakes of real people are banned.
- **For us:** most trend recreations are under a minute and earn nothing from Creator Rewards. TikTok is a reach and brand-deal channel unless we make 60+ second videos.

## Instagram
- **Programs:** Gifts (Stars on Reels), Subscriptions, Live badges, and invite-only Reels bonuses (paid on Reels views over about 30 days). All need a Professional account, age 18+ and an eligible country. Gifts need around 500 followers; Subscriptions and badges around 10,000.
- **AI profiles (since 2026-08-31):** accounts that feature an AI-generated person must use the **"AI-generated profile"** label. Unlabelled ones get their reach cut. Labelled ones aren't penalised for being AI. Posts can also carry "AI info" tags.
- **Originality (April–May 2026):** accounts that mostly repost without material edits lose recommendations and can lose monetization.
- **For us:** turn on "AI-generated profile" from the first post.

## X
- **Creator Revenue Sharing closed:** no new sign-ups from 2026-08-07, earnings until 2026-09-07, final payout around 2026-09-11.
- **Replacement, Original Content Rewards (from 2026-09-08):**
  - Eligibility: X Premium, 500 verified followers, 500,000 Home-timeline impressions from verified users in the last 90 days, an account in good standing, and a review of 10 original posts.
  - Excluded: copies of other creators' work, reposts with only watermarks or captions added, aggregation, **automated posts**, and posts flagged by Community Notes.
  - X's test: "if the post vanished, what would be lost that only that creator supplied".
- **For us:** post manually, with a clear original angle. X is a small earner unless the account grows a verified (Premium) audience.

## Routes that work regardless of country
These are legitimate. Faking a location doesn't work: every program verifies identity, tax details and the payout bank account, and a mismatch gets the account banned with earnings clawed back.
1. **YouTube first:** it's already available from Tunisia.
2. **A partner who really lives in an eligible country** owns the accounts and payouts, under a written revenue-share contract. Agencies and channel managers commonly work this way.
3. **Legal residence abroad** (for example studying in the EU) makes TikTok, Instagram and X eligible.
4. **Revenue without country limits:** brand deals, sponsorships, affiliate links, UGC work. For AI influencers these usually earn more than creator funds.

## What the Socials page should check per post (later)
- Realistic AI disclosed (YouTube's synthetic box, TikTok's AI label, Instagram's AI-generated profile and AI info).
- Original take, not a re-upload (our "adaptation idea" exists).
- TikTok: is it over 60 s if it's meant for Creator Rewards?
- No sensitive topics (health, finance) voiced by the AI persona on YouTube.
- X: posted by hand (automated posts are excluded).
